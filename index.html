Saving 1 · Every day

Free power at midday

12 PM – 3 PMLocal time

  • Your battery usually charges from solar only. If solar is not enough, use this free time to charge it from the grid. Set a schedule (timer) for this.

  • Run your big appliances now: washing machine, dryer and dishwasher. Also hot water or a pool pump on a timer, and charging an electric car.

  • Charge your battery in any weather, so it is ready to run your home on your own stored power.

Free up to 50 kWh a day. After that, a small cost.
Saving 2 · Every day

Earn $1 a day

6 PM – 9 PMUse your battery,
stay off the grid

  • If your battery covers your total home usage from 6pm to 9pm, we pay you $1 a day. It is automatic, with nothing to set up.

  • It is designed to help offset your daily supply charge, the fixed amount on your bill.

  • A tiny trickle from the grid is normal and OK: up to 0.03 kWh each hour.

  • Your battery can only give so much power at one time. If many big appliances are on together, the extra may come from the grid. Check your battery's app to see how much power (kW) it can give.

Up to $365 a year

Battery setup

Set up your battery timer

Your battery will not charge from the grid by itself. To get free charging and to sell your excess power, it needs to be programmed (scheduled).

The $1 a day is automatic. There is nothing to set up for it.

What to set

12pm – 3pmCharge from the grid, up to 100%
6pm – 9pmOptional: sell your excess power

Using our app

  • Our app takes over scheduling from your battery's own app.

  • 3 modes: Self-consumption (default), Schedule (you set the times), or Auto (we charge it for you, and can also sell within limits you set).

Getting the most from it

Tips and help

Your day at a glance

12pm – 3pmFree power: charge the battery, run big appliances
6pm – 9pmStay on battery for $1 a day. Optional: sell for 10c

Tips to avoid buying grid power

  • Stay under your battery's limit. If the oven, kettle, heater and air con run together, the extra may come from the grid. Spread big jobs out.

  • Do not run your battery flat. Run the washing machine, dryer and dishwasher in the free 12pm to 3pm window instead of the evening.

Watch for Critical Events. On rare days when prices spike, our app may ask you to sell or charge for about 5 minutes. Do it and get rewarded ($1 per kWh to sell, 5c per kWh to charge). It is optional.
